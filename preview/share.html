The longer versionFacebook, Instagram captions, email, group chats
Portland's council and county races don't have party labels. But the money tells a story.

Who's Buying PDX checked every itemized individual donor to six business-backed candidates against up to 20 years of Oregon campaign filings. 128 of them have also given to Republican campaigns, including 39 to Christine Drazan. Run the same test on the progressive councilors running against them and the count is zero.

These candidates are Democrats. That's the point: Republicans can't win here, so their story gets told by Democrats who can.

Every number links to a public record. See who's buying: whosbuyingpdx.com

Ground rules

Keep it accurate.

  • Stick to the numbers on the site, and link the page they come from. Every claim here has a source.
  • Don't name individual donors. We withhold names on purpose. The point is the pattern, not any one person.
  • Don't call the candidates Republicans. They're Democrats. Say "business-backed" or "Portland conservatives," and see what we mean.
  • Keep whosbuyingpdx.com on the graphics; don't crop it off. Every number traces back to the site.
  • Remind people how ranked-choice works: anyone you rank can end up with your vote, so only rank candidates you'd want to win.
  • Spot an error? Tell us. We'll correct it and post the date on Receipts.
